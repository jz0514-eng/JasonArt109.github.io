About Me

Bio

Jason Zheng is a student at San José State University, majoring in Digital Media Art with a focus on UI/UX design. He is interested in how digital media expresses different ideas and messages with design. Jason aims to created dynamic, accessible website for everyone. dynamic interface, streamlined design, accessibility/user centered

Artist Statement

The goal of my works is to explore how digital design can change how people experience and understand information, my goal is to create a website that is both visually engaging and accessible. With UI/UX design and CSS/Javascript, I aim to create website with both functionality and creativity, creating a digital space that's dynamic and interactive while still being user centered.
